## RAGTool
Topic-consistent support for convolution output size:
- The output size is computed independently for height and width.
- The general formula is O = floor((N + 2P - K) / S) + 1.
- N is the input size, K is the kernel size, P is padding, and S is stride.
- Valid convolution normally uses zero padding, while same convolution chooses padding to preserve the intended spatial size.
